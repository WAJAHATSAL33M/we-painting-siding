# Service-page local SEO research and implementation

Reviewed: 17 October 2026

Scope: 16 sub-service pages and two main service pages. Public search-result sampling was used to identify relevant service, material, preparation, cost and location intents. No Search Console, Keyword Planner, paid keyword database or verified search-volume dataset was available. These are intent-led targets, not a ranked list of highest-volume keywords.

Main pages address category-wide planning; sub-service pages address the specific surface or task. Existing service-area links remain. Local paragraphs link to relevant listed city pages without creating more city/service doorway pages.

## Editorial rules

- Unique introductions, overview paragraphs, local planning copy, estimate factors and service-specific FAQs.
- Preserve the approved section hierarchy; use real, visible text and natural paragraph breaks.
- Use concise, unique titles and descriptions. No arbitrary power-word, number, keyword-density or word-count targets.
- No copied competitor prices, warranties, reviews, experience claims, office addresses or completed-project claims.
- Describe image contents; do not add city names to photographs with unverified locations.
- Keep Service and Breadcrumb structured data consistent with visible content. Do not fabricate a LocalBusiness address or review ratings.
- Google can select different title links or snippets; on-page changes do not guarantee rankings, indexing or local-pack placement.
