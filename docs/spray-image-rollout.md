## New generated assets

Built-in image generation was used. Every prompt requested a 1536 × 1024 photorealistic editorial scene, realistic hose-fed airless equipment, controlled spray, protected adjacent surfaces and suitable protective equipment; no brushes, rollers, logos, text or watermarks. ## Installed-door correction

Cabinet and interior door process photos now show doors attached in the home. The two detached-door generated assets are removed. Legacy source paths resolve to the installed-door replacements, including the archived loose-door preparation reference. Prompts require attached hinges/frames or complete installed cabinetry, protected adjacent surfaces, professional spray equipment and PPE; prohibit detached panels, stands, sawhorses, booths, brushes, rollers, text and logos.
